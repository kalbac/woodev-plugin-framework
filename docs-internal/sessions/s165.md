## Operator decisions

- **Rig demo data deleted** (orders 1046/1047, method 283, product 1045, option).
- **Webhooks use Tailscale Funnel**, not Postman or ngrok. Router and tailnet config are never touched.
- **All CDEK webhook types go into the v2 release:**
  - a CDEK price change gives a note + flag + hook and never rewrites WC totals;
  - the framework neutral-events card comes after step 2;
  - OFFICE_AVAILABILITY goes last, after measuring its volume;
  - on the production account, subscriptions are only listed (read-only).
- **Subscription cap: 2 per TYPE per account.**
  - Check slots before subscribing.
  - «Тестовый сайт ≠ тестовый контур»: a test site with production keys takes a slot.
  - Webhooks are OPT-IN, with «Подключить» / «Отключить» buttons and the result shown at once.
  - Never enabled on localhost.
  - Foreign subscriptions are never deleted.
- **Settings layout:**
  - options are grouped into cards inside a section;
  - the sender belongs with the seller;
  - the webhook card goes to «Выгрузка заказов» next to «Статусы доставки», not to «Дополнительно»;
  - «Статус доставленного заказа» moves to «Статусы доставки».
- **Cancelling in WC is never blocked** (the admin trusts the merchant). An in-transit parcel gets a clear note and an explicit «Оформить отказ» (#1204).
