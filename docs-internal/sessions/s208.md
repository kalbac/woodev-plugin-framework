## #1534 — what shipped in the framework

- `mayEnterChain()` (`location-cascade.js`) now lets a DaData `address` record through when it names the settlement confirmed by
  another provider; `Customer_Location_Store::rebuild_chain()` keeps the stored CDEK settlement as a **foreign settlement anchor**
  (`foreign_settlement_anchor()`) under the house. Result: chain `{cdek region, cdek settlement, dadata address}`, current = address.
  CDEK, Yandex and Post read `get_customer_record_at( 'settlement' )` first and are untouched; Ozon's courier reads the current record
  and gets the house.
- The cross-provider proof is `Location_Record::is_same_locality_as()` (country + settlement name + a **region** check). Critic rounds:
  R1 (sol) — 2 Must: a same-named city in another region (`Благовещенск` Amur vs Bashkortostan) was accepted; a nested settlement
  (`Пушкино` / `Черкизово`) failed the proof. R2 (luna) — 1 Must: a region label with a spaced em dash vs an ASCII hyphen
  (`Ханты-Мансийский … — Югра` vs `… - Югра`) did not match; fixed in PHP and JS (a dash surrounded by whitespace is a separator, an
  internal hyphen is kept). R3 — ship.
- A hand-crafted POST of an SPb house under a confirmed Москва settlement replaces the chain; it is not reachable through the UI
  (the field returns no suggestions for another city) — noted, not a card.
- The first-pass report chose "regions are NOT compared"; critic R1 overturned that, and the shipped rule does compare them.

## The rejected critic finding (Ozon #71, round 2)

Luna R2 Must: with a DaData house `settlement=Пушкино`, `nested_settlement=Черкизово` and the buyer having confirmed «Пушкино», the
courier snapshot exports «Черкизово», not the confirmed city. **Not accepted by the coordinator:** the house record itself states it
lies in Черкизово, and the snapshot carries that house's coordinates, street and number, so the house's deepest locality is the factual
shipment city; exporting the outer city is exactly the round-1 defect. The level picked in the city field does not move the house.
Recorded on the PR (2 rounds of the 3-round cap). The reason for rejecting a finding goes on the PR, not into silence.
